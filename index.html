The Process

HOW IT WORKS

A streamlined flow from donor registration to blood delivery — automated and transparent.

01

Donor Registers & Gets Screened

A donor signs up with personal details and blood type. The system checks eligibility based on age, weight, last donation date, and health conditions before approving them.

02

Blood is Donated & Processed

Approved donors schedule an appointment. Post-donation, the blood is logged into inventory with a unique unit ID, blood type, quantity, and expiry date.

03

Hospital Submits a Request

A hospital or patient creates a blood request with blood type, quantity needed, and urgency level. The system scans inventory in real time for matching units.

04

Admin Reviews & Approves

The blood bank administrator reviews the request, confirms available inventory, and dispatches the units. The system updates stock automatically.

05

Blood Delivered, Life Saved

The hospital receives confirmation and the blood units. Transaction history is logged for audit. Donors are notified their contribution made a difference.
